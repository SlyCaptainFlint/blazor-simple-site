Olga Zinoveva

My name is Olga. I live in Seattle with my husband, my son, and a parrot who is as beautiful as he is cantankerous. I am a software developer who's done the tour of Seattle tech big and small, currently serving as the CTO of an interactive fiction startup called Glimmerfics.

I started my career in Xbox, working on the Halo franchise for five years. I remember my Halo team as the most passionate, quirky, and interesting people I've ever worked with. My reasons for leaving the industry were many, but I can't say that I've never looked back and wondered what it would be like to try game development again. I am still a huge Halo fan and gamer in general.

After Xbox, I have done a bit of everything - founded my own startup (AllMyGames), worked for other startups (Bright Machines), worked in big tech (Microsoft and Meta). I love to build things - be they objects I model and 3D print, pieces of art I create when fancy strikes, or that ephemeral thing we call software.

Photography

I love all visual art, but photography has a special place in my heart. I got my first DSLR right after college; over time, I have upgraded my hardware and the software I use to process photos, but the biggest upgrade by far has been to my skill. I am no expert by any means, but I'm always getting better and looking for a new perspective. I most enjoy capturing the beauty of landscapes - natural and man-made.

About this site

I made my first personal page on the Internet when I was 12. A virtual pet website, Neopets, allowed customizing the CSS on the pet pages, and having a beautiful one was a point of pride for serious players.

The internet has changed since then; gone are the days of tiled textures and visitor counters. The marquee tag packed its bindle and rode off into the sunset of obsolescence. I forgot my Neopets password. So, in 2019, it was time for a new personal page!

The original iteration of this site was built with Blazor, but it ultimately did quite little, powering just routing and a bit of state management. The newest iteration uses vanilla TypeScript and Vite. The background is SVG-driven, based on a template from Animoplex. All source code is available on GitHub.
